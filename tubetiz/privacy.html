Privacy policy

Tubetiz: NoneOfYa! Vault · Last updated November 5, 2026

Short version: we don't collect anything. Your photos, videos and settings stay on your iPhone, encrypted, and never reach us or anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting, and it makes no network requests.

Your photos and videos

Everything you add is encrypted on your device with AES-256 using a key protected by your password. Your password is never stored. The vault is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. We have no way to see, recover or reset your vault.

Permissions

Photos: to import the photos you pick, save items back when you ask, and delete originals only when you confirm. If you use Find, the app reads your photos on your iPhone to spot cards, IDs, passwords and documents. That reading happens entirely on the device; nothing is uploaded or stored, and the results are forgotten when the vault locks. Location data is removed from photos as they come into the vault and when you share or save them (you can turn this off in Settings).
Camera and microphone: to take photos and videos inside the vault, and, only if you turn on Break-in Photos, to take one photo after a wrong password. Break-in photos are encrypted and stay on your device.
Face ID: to unlock the vault, if you turn it on. Face data is handled by iOS and never seen by the app.

Backups and sharing

Backups you create are encrypted files that you save wherever you choose. When you share or save an item, it goes only where you send it.

Kids

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
