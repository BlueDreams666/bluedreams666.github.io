Getting around

  • Spin to tune: drag the globe and stop with a dot inside the ring. That place's top station starts playing.
  • Numbered dots are several places close together. Click one to zoom in until they split apart.
  • Surprise me flies you to a random place and plays a station there.
  • The star next to a station saves it to Favourites.

When something's off

A station says it's offline.

Stations go off the air, move their stream or have a bad day, and the directory doesn't always catch up straight away. Orbit Radio marks them and, inside a place, moves on to the next station for you. Try again later, or pick another one.

The globe says it couldn't load the stations.

Check your internet connection and click Try again. The station list is saved after the first download, so after that the globe shows up even when the directory is slow.

I hear nothing, but it says Live.

Check the volume slider under the station name and your Mac's own volume. Some stations also play a few seconds of silence while they connect.

The globe keeps changing station while I move it.

That's Spin to tune. Click the Spin to tune button at the top left of the globe to turn it off, or switch it off in Orbit Radio › Settings.

I don't want the radio icon in my menu bar.

Turn off Show in the menu bar in Orbit Radio › Settings.

The app is in Hebrew, or English, and I want the other.

Orbit Radio follows your Mac's language. To set it for Orbit Radio only, go to System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications.

A station I like is missing.

Orbit Radio shows the stations in the Radio Browser directory. Anyone can add a station there, and it'll show up in Orbit Radio within a day.
