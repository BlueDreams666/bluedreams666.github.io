Privacy

Orbit Radio collects nothing about you. Here's the whole story. Last updated 4 November 2026.

What I collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and I don't receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your Mac

Your favourites, recently played stations, volume and settings are saved on your Mac only. They're never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

Who the app talks to

  • Radio stations. When you play a station, your Mac connects straight to that broadcaster's server, like any internet radio. The broadcaster can see your IP address, as any website you visit can. Each station is run by its own broadcaster.
  • Radio Browser, the free station directory. Orbit Radio downloads the station list and searches from it. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Apple Maps. The globe and town names come from Apple. To name a town, the app sends Apple the location of that station on the map, never yours. Orbit Radio never asks for your location.

Kids

Orbit Radio doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. Stations are live broadcasts the app can't filter, so some may not suit younger listeners.

If this changes

If this ever changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
