Spin the globe. Hear the world.

Orbit Radio puts thousands of live radio stations on a spinning globe. Turn it, stop on a glowing dot, and you're listening to whatever is on the air there right now.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For the Mac, macOS 14 or later. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

Drag the globe and the station under the ring starts playing. Keep spinning and you're channel-surfing the planet: morning news in Lisbon, late-night jazz in Tokyo, a gospel hour in Florida.

Hit Surprise me and it flies you somewhere random and plays what's on there. Open a place and you'll see its local time, so you know when you're catching someone's night shift.

And the everyday stuff

  • Search by name, genre or region, with the top stations in your country ready to go
  • Favourites and recently played
  • A menu bar shortcut, your keyboard's play/pause key, and a sleep timer
  • Stations that are off the air get spotted and skipped
  • English and Hebrew, light and dark mode, VoiceOver

The stations come from Radio Browser, a free directory run by its community. Each station belongs to its own broadcaster.
